---
description: Use when someone asks for an overall recap, scorecard, summary or "how are our events doing", or wants a recommendation on which events to repeat or cut.
---

1. Call `list_events` sorted by `closedWonUsd`, then `conversion_stats`, then `bd_leaderboard`.
2. Open with one line: total events, total budget, total closed-won value and the overall close rate.
3. One bullet per event, best first: closed-won value, open pipeline, pipeline per $1 of budget, leads.
4. Call out the standout: the best event on revenue and the best on value for money. Say if they differ.
5. Call out risk: open deals with no recent action and the largest open deals, with their owners.
6. Finish with a recommendation: which format to run again and one deal to chase this week.
